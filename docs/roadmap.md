# Planned for v0.3

v0.2 is complete. These items are planned, not promised. Each one keeps the project's rules:

- free sources only, with their licences respected;
- no scraping;
- standard-library Python and a static page;
- every new record starts as unreviewed.

## Live reporting

- A way for people to report an AI harm as it happens, **only** if a safe and lawful route can be agreed with a
  relevant authority. Victims must still be sent to 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in first.
- Until then, the site stays a record of public sources, plus the *Suggest a correction* and *Send to NETRA for
  review* forms.

## MIT AI Risk Repository import

- Import the MIT AI Risk Repository's risk entries and incident links (CC BY 4.0, with attribution), kept in their own
  file and mapped to NETRA's 24 risk areas.
- The repository's layout, branding, text and code are not copied. NETRA's 14-stage structure stays as it is.
